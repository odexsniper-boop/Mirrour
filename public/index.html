How the engine works

          
Every copied trade passes six steps, then the exit engine re-checks five rules every 2 seconds.

        

      


      

        
6-step copy pipeline

        
    
          
  1. 1
    Watch
    The Solana wallets you follow, read live from the blockchain.
    2. 
          
  2. 2
    Filter
    Entry filters and token safety audit.
    3. 
          
  3. 3
    Size
    Fixed $, % of balance, or proportional 1:1.
    4. 
          
  4. 4
    Verify
    Slippage and the 25% max-chase guard.
    5. 
          
  5. 5
    Execute
    Paper trade filled at the live market price (no real funds).
    6. 
          
  6. 6
    Exit
    Hand-off to the 5-rule auto-exit engine.
    7. 
        

      


      

        

          
5-rule auto-exit
Checked every 2s

          

            
1
Take Initials
At a target gain (e.g. +25%), sell the main chunk (75%) and keep a moonbag.

            
2
Moonbag Trail
Trailing stop off the peak (e.g. 25%), armed once the position is up +10%.

            
3
Two-Stage Floor
A wide opening stop (−45%) that tightens to −20% after 50 seconds.

            
4
Wave-Cut
Exit after 90 seconds if the peak never reached +5%.

            
5
Hard Time Cap
Close the main position after 300 seconds, leaving the moonbag.

          

        

        

          
Execution protection

          

            
Max Chase (25%)
Compares your quote to the leader's fill and skips if the price already ran past the threshold.

            
Honeypot block
Skips tokens flagged as honeypots (tokens that cannot be sold).

            
Freeze / mint authority block
Skips Solana tokens whose freeze or mint authority is still active.
